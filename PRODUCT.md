# Folio
Personal portfolio tracking and investment research for an investor with US assets at INDmoney and Indian equities and funds at Upstox. The user supplied the detailed brief and two visual references. Portfolio clarity, transparent data provenance, accurate currency treatment, and graceful missing-data states are core requirements. Never execute trades. Demo data is isolated and explicitly labeled. Production accounts begin empty.

Web application for desktop, tablet, and phone. Core journey: try demo or register, connect/import, understand portfolio, research securities, maintain watchlists, and ask evidence-grounded questions. Live features require the user's provider credentials and authorized upstream access; credentials stay on the server.
